Views collapsing? Before blaming a shadowban, know that most drops come from the content, not a hidden penalty.

Check in 1 minute

On Instagram, open your profile, the menu, then "Account Status". It tells you whether your account can be recommended, and lets you appeal. Real restrictions mostly concern unoriginal content and rule violations.

The most common causes

  • A weaker hook: people leave in the first seconds.
  • A change of topic: the platform has to find a new audience.
  • Reposts or videos with another app's watermark.
  • The comparison effect after a viral video.

Avoid deleting and reposting over and over, or buying followers. Fake accounts skew your analytics.

Since December 2025, Instagram limits every post and Reel to 5 hashtags. Lists of 30 hashtags are over.

What they're still for

A hashtag is a label: it helps categorise your content, it doesn't push it. Adam Mosseri has said hashtags don't increase reach. Viewer reactions are what count.

How to choose your 5 hashtags

  • Specific rather than generic: #veganrecipe rather than #food.
  • One format hashtag (#quickrecipe) and, if useful, a local one (#london).
  • Tap each one: if the posts shown have nothing to do with your topic, change it.

Put your effort into the caption and on-screen text instead, using the words people type into search.

Instagram hashtags: the 5-hashtag limit and what they're still for

No more lists of 30 hashtags. Here's how to use them now that Instagram allows 5.

Updated October 24, 2026

Since December 2025, Instagram limits every post and Reel to 5 hashtags. The days of 30-hashtag lists are over.

What they're still for

A hashtag is a label: it helps categorize your content, it doesn't push it. Adam Mosseri has said hashtags don't increase reach. Viewer reactions are what count.

How to choose your 5 hashtags

  • Specific rather than generic: #veganrecipe rather than #food.
  • One format hashtag (#quickrecipe) and, if useful, a local one (#london).
  • Tap each one: if the posts shown have nothing to do with your topic, change it.

Put your effort into the caption and on-screen text instead, using the words people type into search.

TikTok algorithm: how the For You feed works

TikTok has published the broad outline of its recommendations. One sentence should reassure every small account.

Updated October 24, 2026

The For You feed doesn't mainly show accounts you follow: it picks videos it thinks you'll enjoy, wherever they come from.

What TikTok has officially said

  1. Interactions: videos liked or shared, accounts followed, comments.
  2. Video information: captions, sounds, hashtags.
  3. Language, country and device, which weigh less.

Watching a video to the end is a strong signal. And above all: neither follower count nor past hits are direct factors. Your next video can take off, even from a small account.

What to do

Open each video's retention curve. If it drops in the first 2 seconds, change the hook. If it declines steadily, cut the dead time.

Say your topic out loud and write it on screen: TikTok will find the right audience more easily.

Source: TikTok Newsroom, "How TikTok recommends videos #ForYou".

Instagram algorithm: the 3 signals that really matter

Instagram's head named the three most important signals. Here's what they mean for your Reels and carousels.

Updated October 24, 2026

In January 2025, Instagram head Adam Mosseri named the three signals that matter most for ranking: watch time, likes and sends.

The three signals

  • Watch time: the longer people stay, the stronger the signal.
  • Likes per reach: these matter most with your followers.
  • Sends per reach: when someone DMs your post to a friend. This is the signal that helps most to reach non-followers.

What to do

Before you film, ask: who would someone send this Reel to? Tips worth keeping, relatable situations and ideas to do together get sent the most.

Read your numbers as ratios: 100 likes from 1,000 accounts reached beat 300 from 30,000.

Watch originality: since April 2026, Instagram no longer recommends accounts that mostly repost other people's content, even with a watermark or credit.
